# Demo script — 6 minutes, four logins

The story is one season at The Royal Knot, told by the four people who sign
in: the **couple** it is all for, the **founder** who runs the agency, a
**manager** who runs the weddings she hands him, and a **vendor** who used to
be chased on WhatsApp. **Do not tour the menu** — each screen answers a
problem the brief raised.

Before recording: start fresh (`./run.sh`), close other tabs, and keep a
second browser window (or a phone) for the couple. Their two links are printed
in the server log on startup.

---

## 0:00–0:20 · The problem, and the four doors

> "The Royal Knot plans 25 luxury weddings a season. Enquiries get forgotten,
> quotes are compared by hand at midnight, vendors are chased by phone, and
> the founder only learns whether a wedding made money months later."

Start on the **sign-in screen**. Click through the four doors — plum,
midnight, jade, blush.

> "Three people run a wedding and one couple has it, so there are four control
> panels. Each one only opens what that person needs."

---

## 0:20–1:20 · Couple — enquire, follow, sign

Click the **Couple** door → **Start an enquiry**. Go through the five steps
quickly: two names, a date, the guest slider, tap a mood palette and Haldi,
the budget slider (point at the per-guest figure), a password. **Send.**

> "That is the brief's first scene — an enquiry that cannot be lost. It's in
> the pipeline with an owner, the manager carrying least, and the couple is
> already in their own portal watching it move."

Sign out. Couple door → **Nitara** (Krish & Nitara, 70 days out).

> "This is what a booked couple sees. Where they are in the journey, a
> countdown to their Mehendi, their planner one tap away — and only the few
> things they have to do: choose their caterer from the top three, answer
> their planner, approve the décor sketches."

Open **Timeline** (it scrolls to *you are here*), then **Payments** →
*I've paid this* → a UTR.

> "That doesn't mark it paid — the manager confirms it against the bank. The
> couple never sees what the agency pays its vendors, and on the day they see
> the plan, not which vendor is running late."

Open **Questions & complaints** → **New message** → **Complaint**.

> "A complaint goes to their planner and the founder at once."

*(30s, optional: sign in as **Suhani** — agreement not signed yet — and sign
it inside the portal with the sticky Sign bar.)*

---

## 1:20–2:40 · Founder — assign, monitor, edit

Sign in as **Founder**. Land on the Season dashboard: point at the revenue,
the margin and the weddings in red.

> "Which weddings made money — the question nobody could answer. But first,
> the people."

Open **Managers**.

> "Five managers. Isha is at risk — eight tasks late and a client payment
> overdue. Aarav carries the most, has vendors reporting delays, and now a
> complaint from Nitara. Nobody had to tell her this."

Point at the gold strip at the top.

> "Two weddings nobody owns — which means nobody is chasing them."

Click **Assign** on one, pick **Meera** (the card shows she has no active
weddings), add a note, assign.

> "It's Meera's now, and every open task moved with it."

Open **Isha Kapoor**.

> "Everything I'd otherwise have to ask her: which weddings, what's late and by
> how many days, what vendors have flagged, what she finished this week."

Open any wedding → **✎ Edit**.

> "Every record has the same Edit button — weddings, enquiries, tasks,
> payments, run-sheet slots, contracts, vendors, staff. Only the founder can
> change who runs a wedding or what the couple pays."

---

## 2:40–4:00 · Manager — my day

Sign out. **Manager** door → **Aarav Mehta**.

> "Aarav sees only the weddings the founder gave him. Not one more — even by
> typing another wedding's id into the address bar."

Point at the hero.

> "A live countdown to the next thing that matters — Ishaan and Trisha's
> Mehendi, timed from the run sheet. And right now, Manav and Rhea is live:
> the groom's car is stuck — the slot turned red on its own — with the
> vendor's number one tap away."

Scroll to **From your vendors**.

> "The DJ: console held up at the city gate, backup on its way. The florist is
> blocked waiting on a generator. Vendors tell him from their own portal; he
> marks it seen and they know he's on it."

Point at **Your couples are asking**, open **Client queries**, answer Nitara's
complaint and **Send and resolve**.

> "The couple can rate it now — and if they reply, it reopens."

Open **Payments**: *Couple says paid · UTR …* → **Confirm UTR**.

Tick one task in the **Checklist** card.

> "Pending is what's late or stuck, upcoming is what's next, finished is done."

Click the **Caterer** quote request.

> "Four caterers quoted. Saffron is cheapest — and covers 22% of the scope.
> The system ticks the three best by *value*, and that's what the couple got."

**Switch to the couple's window** (the `choose` link from the log). Show
three cards — no "recommended" label. Pick one, confirm with a name and a note.

Back on Aarav's screen, reload.

> "Chosen by the couple, with their note. The fourth quote and the two losers
> closed themselves, and the winner is a contract on this wedding."

*(Optional, 15s: **Payments** — money in from couples and out to vendors on
one screen; overdue in red.)*

---

## 4:00–5:20 · Vendor — price it, report it, earn it

Sign out. **Vendor** door → **Gulmohar Decor Studio**.

> "A decorator. One request waiting for a price, a live job today, and what
> the season is worth to them."

Open the request.

> "The wedding, the dates, 450 guests, the scope, the manager to call, a
> reply-by countdown — and a check against their own calendar and capacity."

Type prices into the starter lines, add a line, enter their own cost.

> "They price it here instead of a PDF. GST, travel, the all-in total the
> manager will see — and, privately, what they keep. That cost never leaves
> this portal."

**Send quote.**

Open **My jobs** → **Update manager** → **Delayed**, a reason and a new date.

> "A delay has to come with a date — 'running late' on its own helps nobody."

Open **Calendar**.

> "Days booked through the agency fill in on their own; they paint the days
> they can't do. When a manager invites decorators, a vendor away on the day
> shows as a conflict — Lake Palace is, for this very wedding."

Open **Earnings**.

> "Contracted, received, outstanding, profit — per job and by month."

---

## 5:20–6:00 · Close the loop

Sign back in as **Aarav**. The décor request now shows Gulmohar's quote in the
comparison, and the delay sits at the top of his vendor updates.

> "Gulmohar's price landed in Aarav's comparison the moment they pressed send —
> same shape as everyone else's. The delay is on his screen in red."

End on the founder's **Season** dashboard.

> "First enquiry to final vendor payment. Four people, four panels, one
> record."

---

## If you have more time

The original six scenes are all still there and each takes 30–40 seconds:
capture an enquiry and send the agreement to a phone to sign (Enquiries); the
deposit generating a 35-task plan (**Deposit received**); the run sheet at
**10:35** going red on its own (live wedding → Run sheet); a bill blocked for
over-billing and refusing to be paid twice (a completed wedding → Money);
60 extra guests raising both sides of a change order (**Reconcile
headcount**); and a revocable share link for the accountant (**Share these
numbers** on Season).

## Recording tips

- 1440×900 or larger; the tables need the width.
- Record the couple's phone steps separately and cut them in.
- Keep the cursor still while talking. Move it only to point at a number.
- Do not read the UI aloud. Say what the screen *means* for the agency.
- The best moments: the enquiry landing the couple straight in their own
  portal, Isha flagged at risk without anyone saying so, the countdown ticking,
  the couple's pick closing four quotes, a complaint reaching the founder, and
  a vendor's price appearing in the manager's comparison. Pause on each.
